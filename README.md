## Signs You May Have a Drain or Sewer Problem

- One drain is slow (one sink, shower or tub draining slowly)
- Several drains are slow at once (a shared pipe may be affected)
- Multiple fixtures backing up together (needs prompt attention)
- One fixture backs up when you run another (a restriction farther down the line)
- Gurgling sounds from a drain
- Foul sewer odors from a drain

If several fixtures back up at once, stop using sinks, toilets, showers and appliances that drain water, keep children and pets away from wastewater, and call us.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What are common causes of slow drains in a home?**
Hair and soap residue can slow bathroom drains, while grease and food debris can restrict kitchen lines. Buildup, roots, or pipe damage may affect a line farther down.

**How do I know whether one drain or the main sewer line is clogged?**
One slow sink or shower often points to that fixture or its branch drain. If several fixtures slow down together, or flushing a toilet makes water rise in a nearby tub, a shared line may be affected. These are clues, not a diagnosis — tell us which fixtures react.

**What can I try for a simple clog?**
A plunger may help with a single affected fixture. If it does not clear, the clog returns, or other drains back up, stop using the affected fixtures and have the problem assessed.

**How often should drains be cleaned?**
There is no fixed schedule for every property. Previous clogs, heavy grease use, root intrusion, and pipe condition can change what maintenance makes sense.
